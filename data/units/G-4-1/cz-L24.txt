<figure class="bkfig"><pre class="geo">O1 = (179.2, 89.3)
O2 = (89.3, 89.3)

k1 = circle(O1, A)
k2 = circle(O2, A)
E = meet(k1, k2, 146.3, 53.4)
C = on(k2, 40.7, 135.9)

<div class="cards">
<div class="card"><h4>① 同弧等角</h4><div class="rule">$\angle CDA=\angle CBA$（同對弧 $AC$）</div>
<div class="eg">外心與高：$\angle BAH=\angle OAC$，都等於 $90^\circ-\angle B$ <span class="pill">換到同一段弧</span></div></div>
<div class="card"><h4>② 直徑 ⇒ 直角</h4><div class="rule">直徑所對圓周角 $=90^\circ$</div>
<div class="eg">延長 $AO$ 到 $D$，$\angle ACD=90^\circ$ <span class="pill">補直徑</span></div></div>
<div class="card"><h4>③ 弦切角</h4><div class="rule">切線與弦夾角 = 對面的圓周角</div>
<div class="eg">兩圓交於 $A$、$E$，切線交角 $\angle BDC=\angle ABE+\angle ACE$，是定值 <span class="pill">切線換圓周角</span></div></div>
</div>
<div class="pitfall"><h4>⚠ 最常錯</h4><ul>
<li>兩個圓周角在弦的<b>兩側</b>時是互補，不是相等</li>
<li>弦切角要對「夾在角裡的那段弧」</li></ul></div>
<div class="bottomline">遇到圓先問：這個角對哪段弧？還有誰也對這段弧？</div>
