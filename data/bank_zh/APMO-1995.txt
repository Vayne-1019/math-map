=== P1
zh: 求所有實數數列 $a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_{1995}$，滿足
$$2\sqrt{a_n - (n-1)} \ge a_{n+1} - (n-1), \quad n = 1, 2, \ldots, 1994,$$
且
$$2\sqrt{a_{1995} - 1994} \ge a_1 + 1.$$
