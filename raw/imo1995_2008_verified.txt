of positive reals with x0 = x1995, such that for i = 1, . . . , 1995,

2. Let ABCDEF be a convex hexagon with AB = BC = CD and DE =
EF = F A, such that ⁄ BCD = ⁄ EF A = π/3. Suppose G and H are
points in the interior of the hexagon such that ⁄ AGB = ⁄ DHE =
2π/3. Prove that AG + GB + GH + DH + HE ≥ CF .
3. Let p be an odd prime number. How many p-element subsets A of
